Skillo

A Chrome side panel that tailors your Overleaf LaTeX resume to a specific job posting, using your own AI provider.

Skillo may reorder, rephrase, condense and cut. It is instructed never to invent an employer, a date, a degree, a certification or a metric that is not already in your resume. You review a diff before anything is written back.
