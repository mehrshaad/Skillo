# Privacy policy

The published policy lives at **<https://ali-dadashzadeh.ir/Skillo/privacy.html>** and its
source is [`docs/privacy.html`](docs/privacy.html) — a single copy, so this file and the
published page can never drift apart.

The short version: Skillo has no servers, collects nothing, and contains no analytics. Your
API keys and your resume stay on your machine. The only place your data is sent is the AI
provider you configure yourself.
